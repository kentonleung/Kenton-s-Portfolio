Project

Parallel Drawing Robot

Co-developed a 2-DOF parallel kinematics robot, acting as the lead software and mechatronics engineer.

View repo

Project Snapshot

  • Role: Software & Mechatronics (Group Project)
  • Tech Stack: MATLAB, Arduino UNO R4, C++, PID Control, Kinematics
  • Hardware: 3D-Print, N20 Gear Motors, L298N H-Bridge, Hall Effect Encoders
  • Outcome: Achieved a 1.2mm PSTD positioning precision rate within a 156x156mm workspace.

The Objective & Hardware Base

Goal: Build a 2-DOF robotic arm to execute precise geometric paths to understand the fundamentals of surgical robotics. We chose a top-down parallel configuration to maximize end-effector accuracy and eliminate gravitational friction on the links.

  • Chassis: Custom-designed and 3D-printed in PLA by the mechanical team.
  • Actuation: Two N20 gear motors mounted perpendicularly using a worm gear transmission.
  • Why Worm Gears? Allowed for a shared origin joint and provided a high torque-to-speed ratio to naturally prevent endpoint overshoot.

Software & Mechatronics (My Role)

While the team handled the physical build and mathematical proofs, I solely owned the mechatronics integration and software control systems.

  • Mechatronics Integration: Wired and programmed the physical feedback loop connecting the N20 motor Hall effect encoders, the L298N motor drivers, and the Arduino microcontroller.
  • Kinematics Translation: Translated the team's theoretical kinematics into a direct analytical trigonometric solution in MATLAB, establishing a real-time serial communication loop with the Arduino.
  • PID Tuning: Manually tuned the Arduino's PID controller, optimizing proportional and derivative gains for rapid motor rise times with zero unstable steady-state error.

Results & Hardware Limitations

We validated the robot via point-to-point navigation and by drawing 50x50mm geometric squares and moving the arm to a desired coordinate.

  • Precision: 1.2–1.32mm PSTD (Highly repeatable positioning).
  • Y-Axis Accuracy: 2.49–3.5mm RMSE (Excellent vertical control).
  • X-Axis Accuracy: 10.74–15.42mm RMSE (Lateral drift).

Engineering Insight: The lateral drift was not a software flaw, but a hardware bottleneck. The mechanical slack in the 3D-printed PLA worm gears caused translation errors. Upgrading to steel gears in future iterations will eliminate this backlash.
