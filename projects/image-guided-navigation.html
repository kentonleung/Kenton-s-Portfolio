Project

Image-Guided Navigation Simulation

Developed a surgical robotics simulation pipeline for safe electrode insertion to the hippocampus.

Back to Portfolio

Overview

This simulation establishes an end-to-end workflow for planning and validating an electrode trajectory through brain anatomy.

Approach

  • Path planning in 3D Slicer.
  • OpenIGTLink data transfer into ROS2.
  • Trajectory constraints to avoid ventricles, vessels and cortex deflection.

Outcome

Calculated and visualized safe electrode trajectories while meeting hard and soft safety constraints for surgical navigation.

Role

Simulation design, medical robotics integration, and safety-aware path planning.

Key constraints

  • Target hippocampus precisely
  • Avoid ventricles and blood vessels
  • Maintain penetration angle above 55°
