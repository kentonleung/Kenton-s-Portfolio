Project

Motion Comparison System

Built a vision-based system to compare motion between two people and compute a similarity score.

Back to Portfolio

Overview

This system captures two subjects performing the same movement and uses computer vision and pose tracking to generate a similarity score.

Approach

  • Depth camera capture with Unity and NuitrackSDK.
  • Pose tracking and post-processing with rule-based scoring.
  • Comparison of motion similarity across subjects.

Outcome

The system successfully differentiated similar and dissimilar motions by producing high and low similarity scores.

Role

Computer vision system design, pose analysis, and motion scoring pipeline.

Key features

  • Depth camera capture
  • Pose tracking data processing
  • Similarity scoring algorithm
